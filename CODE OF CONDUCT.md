# Vayu Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Vayu is an independent programming language project built around learning, experimentation, engineering, and collaboration.

We want the Vayu community to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone who participates in the project.

By participating in the Vayu project, you agree to help maintain this environment.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate toward others.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Welcome people with different levels of programming experience.
- Give useful technical feedback without attacking the person.
- Respect different opinions about language design and implementation.
- Keep discussions focused on improving Vayu and its ecosystem.
- Credit other people's work and ideas when appropriate.
- Report bugs, security issues, and problems responsibly.
- Be patient when helping beginners or reviewing contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in Vayu project spaces:

- Harassment, bullying, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on personal characteristics or background.
- Hate speech or deliberately demeaning language.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private or sensitive information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Impersonating project contributors or misrepresenting project decisions.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, deceptive contributions, or harmful content.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the community hostile or unsafe.

## Technical Discussions

Vayu is a language-design and software-development project. Strong technical disagreement is allowed and encouraged when it helps improve the project.

When disagreeing:

1. Explain the technical reasoning behind your position.
2. Focus on the proposal, code, design, or evidence rather than the person.
3. Prefer reproducible examples and benchmarks over personal claims.
4. Accept that maintainers may make a different final decision.

A rejected idea is not a rejected person.

## Contributions

Contributors should make a reasonable effort to ensure that their contributions are original, properly attributed, and compatible with the project's licensing requirements.

Do not intentionally introduce malicious behavior, hidden backdoors, or unrelated harmful changes into Vayu or its ecosystem.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the Vayu project maintainer through the project's available GitHub contact or communication channel rather than escalating the situation publicly.

For security vulnerabilities, avoid posting sensitive vulnerability details in a public issue. Report them privately when a private security-reporting channel is available.

Reports should include enough context to understand what happened, while avoiding unnecessary private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action to protect the community and project spaces. Depending on the situation, actions may include:

- Asking someone to stop or change their behavior.
- Removing inappropriate comments or contributions.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation in project spaces.

Actions will be based on the circumstances and severity of the behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official Vayu project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue discussions, pull requests, documentation discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the Vayu project or community.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether something is appropriate, choose the more respectful and constructive approach, or ask a project maintainer before proceeding.
